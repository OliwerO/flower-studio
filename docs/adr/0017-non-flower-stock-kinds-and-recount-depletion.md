# Non-flower stock lives in `stock` under a row-level kind, and Materials and Supplies deplete only by Recount

Non-flower goods become Stock Items in the existing `stock` table, discriminated by a new row-level `stock_kind` column with four values: `flower`, `material`, `addon`, `supply`. An **Add-on** (vase, decoration) behaves exactly like a flower — it sits on an Order Line, decrements exactly, and earns revenue. A **Material** (foam, ribbon, wrapping paper) and a **Supply** (trash bags, toilet paper) never touch an Order Line at all: their quantity falls only at a **Recount**, a Florist-initiated correction to physical reality, recorded in a new `stock_recount_log`.

## Why

The Owner needs two things from non-flower goods that she cannot get today: knowing when to re-buy them, and knowing what she spends on them. Both need a quantity that depletes; neither needs it to be exact.

Per-bouquet logging would give exact numbers and was rejected outright — a Florist will not record "1 box, 40 cm of ribbon" on every arrangement, and a count nobody maintains is worse than no count, because it looks authoritative. Recount-only accepts a number that is stale between counts in exchange for one the Florist will actually keep true. The Owner already runs physical counts roughly weekly, so the workflow exists; this only gives it somewhere to land.

Recount-only then decides the money question by itself. With no per-Order attribution, a Material's cost **cannot** be per-Order COGS — it is a period cost, the same shape as rent or marketing. Add-ons are the exception precisely because the customer pays for them: revenue demands exact attribution, and the Owner is entering them at order creation anyway.

[ADR-0006](0006-variety-four-tuple-identity.md) already reserved this ground, noting that `Type=Vase, Colour=Clear, Size=null, Cultivar=null` is a valid Variety and that non-flowers need no schema rework. This ADR takes that option up and adds the one thing 0006 did not anticipate: that non-flowers need to be *told apart* from flowers by every consumer.

## Considered alternatives

- **A separate `materials` table.** Clean isolation, and no risk of polluting flower metrics. Rejected because it duplicates the entire Y-model — dated Batches, FEFO, receiving, write-offs, the trace surface, Stock Order lines — for goods that are bought and counted exactly like flowers are.
- **A real `varieties` table**, with `stock_kind` on it, so the kind physically cannot diverge between two Batches of the same ribbon. This is the structurally correct answer and it would also fix [#597](https://github.com/OliwerO/flower-studio/issues/597) and [#562](https://github.com/OliwerO/flower-studio/issues/562). Rejected for scope: ADR-0006 already weighed and rejected a "Variety registry table", and it is a live migration of the `stock` table — the last such cutover (#291) ran months. It remains a legitimate future refactor, which would supersede ADR-0006 and this ADR's row-level choice.
- **Calibrated auto-deduction** — measure once how much wrap a bouquet consumes, then deduct that rate automatically per order. Gives a live rebuy signal at zero Florist burden. Rejected by the Owner in favour of Recount-only: with a weekly Recount overriding the counter anyway, the deduction adds a moving part whose error the Recount silently erases.
- **Reusing the existing `category` column** as the discriminator. No migration. Rejected on evidence: 102 of 112 production rows hold `'Other'` and the rest hold flower *types* (`'Roses'`, `'Hydrangeas'`). It is unconstrained free text that has already re-polluted once. `category` is deprecated by this ADR, not cleaned.
- **Deriving the kind from `unit`** (flowers are `'Stems'`). Rejected: 43 of 112 production rows have a NULL unit, so it fails on a third of live data.

## Consequences

- `stock` gains `stock_kind`, NOT NULL with a constrained value set, backfilled to `flower` for all existing rows. `category` is no longer read.
- **The kind must propagate like a Variety attribute.** Because it lives on the row, two Batches of one Material can disagree, which is pitfall `batch-variety-attrs` (#323) in a new costume. A 2026-08-01 audit confirmed three sites that already fabricate stock rows with hardcoded flower assumptions and must carry the kind forward: `stockOrderService.js:387` (mints a card literally declaring `Unit = 'Stems'`), `stockOrderService.js:209`, and `stockPurchases.js:52` (both stamp `'Stems'` onto Batches of unit-less cards). Needs a regression test asserting a received Batch inherits its origin's kind.
- **Pickers filter to `flower` and `addon`.** A Material or Supply must never be selectable in a bouquet builder, an order wizard, or an allocation picker.
- **Analytics must exclude non-flowers, and the fix is two-sided.** Materials and Supplies inflate `calculateInventoryTurnover`'s denominator while never reaching its numerator, so the flower turnover KPI drifts down forever; they also corrupt waste %, the supplier scorecard, and stock-loss totals. Symmetrically, **Add-ons corrupt the numerator** — a vase's sell price lands in `flowerRevenue` and its cost in `paidFlowerCost` (`analyticsService.js:267`). Filtering only the stock side skews the ratio the other way.
- **Ask Blossom and Explorer need the kind exposed.** Neither `dataQueryPack.js` nor `explorerSchema.js` can currently express "flowers only", and the Explorer tab is labelled "Stock (flowers)". Without the field the assistant will narrate 40 packs of foam as "40 stems".
- **Recount is not a Write-off.** A Write-off names a cause and feeds waste reporting; a Recount claims no cause and records drift. They are kept in separate tables so counter drift never appears as waste. A Recount drains Batches FEFO on a shortfall; a surplus creates a new Batch dated today at cost 0, flagged recount-origin, rather than inflating an existing Batch's cost basis.
- **Quantities stay integer.** `current_quantity` is unchanged; a Continuous Material (paper, ribbon on the roll) is stored in an integer base unit and Recounted as whole packs plus a rough fraction of the open one, multiplied by `Lot Size`. The fraction is an estimate by design.
- **The rebuy loop needs thresholds.** `buildPoSuggestions` is demand-driven only (`committed > 0`), and Materials have no Demand Entries, so they can never surface there. A second branch keys off `reorder_threshold` for non-flower kinds. Since that field is set on **0 of 112** production rows today, a threshold is required when creating a Material or Supply — otherwise the loop stays as dormant as it is now.
- **One shopping run, not two.** Material and Supply lines appear on the same Stock Order the Driver shops. The evaluation stage (a Florist marking damaged stems) is meaningless for bin bags and is skipped by kind. The receive path's absorption maths also assumes negative quantity means unmet demand (`stockOrderService.js:194`) — for a recount-only kind a negative can only mean a Recount overshot, so absorption must not apply.
